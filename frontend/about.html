How It Works

📦 Borrowing Gear

  1. Go to the locker in the sports centre
  2. Log into gear.thomascreagh.com
  3. Open My Loans on the site
  4. Select which locker(s) you need T Wall for a day on the wall, Outdoor if you're heading out to Dalkey or elsewhere outdoors
  5. Enter the verification code shown on the locker
  6. Open it and take what you need
  7. In My Loans, open that loan and enter each item's code in the items section

↩️ Returning Gear

  1. Go to the locker in the sports centre
  2. Log into gear.thomascreagh.com
  3. Open My Loans on the site
  4. Go to the loan that you are returning
  5. Go to Step 3 in your loan and click the "Get return codes"
  6. Enter the verification code shown on the locker
  7. Open it and place the gear back neatly 🙏
  8. Take a photo of each locker you used
  9. In My Loans, upload the photos to that loan and click Return
